Scrivener for Mac

Scrivener is a project management and writing tool for writers of all kinds. It stays with you from that first unformed idea all the way through to the first—or even final—draft. Outline and structure your ideas. Take notes. Storyboard your masterpiece using a powerful virtual corkboard. View research while you write. Track themes using keywords. Dynamically combine multiple scenes into a single text. Already adopted by best-selling novelists and novices alike.

Outline and Structure

Organize your thoughts with flexible outlining tools. Break down complex projects into manageable sections, rearrange with drag-and-drop, and see your structure at a glance.

Virtual Corkboard

Storyboard visually on a digital corkboard. Pin index cards for scenes, characters, or plot points. Shift them around to refine your narrative flow effortlessly.

Research and Writing

Keep research materials integrated. Import documents, images, and web pages directly into your project. Reference them side-by-side while drafting.

Keywords and Themes

Tag elements with keywords to track themes, characters, and motifs across your manuscript. Generate reports to analyze patterns and consistency.

Dynamic Composition

Compile scenes into a single draft on demand. Customize formatting for export to any standard, from manuscripts to ebooks.

How It Works

Step 1: Collect Ideas

Start a new project. Import notes, research, and outlines. Use the binder to organize everything in one place.

Step 2: Storyboard and Plan

Switch to Corkboard or Outliner view. Arrange index cards or outline items to build your structure visually.

Step 3: Write and Refine

Draft in the editor with split views for reference. Apply keywords to track progress and themes.

Step 4: Compile and Export

Combine sections into a polished draft. Export to Word, PDF, or ePub with professional formatting.
